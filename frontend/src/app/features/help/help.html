Common questions

Can I try a lesson before creating an account?

Yes. Choose a language in the course catalog, then select “Try a lesson without signing up”. Read and hear the new words before answering a few questions. Trials award no XP and reset when you leave.

Where should a complete beginner start?

Open your course and choose “Start from scratch”. Meet the words before practising them. If you already know some of the language, choose the level check instead. Your daily goal is a study target, not a timer that keeps a lesson open.

I cannot sign in or confirm my email.

Use the address you registered with and check your spam folder for the latest confirmation message. You normally confirm your address once. If the link is unavailable, request another one. Never send your password or security code to support.

How do I hear a word more clearly?

Select Listen to hear a word or sentence at its normal speed, or Slowly for a slower replay. Selecting a word option also plays its pronunciation when a recording is available. Check your device volume if you cannot hear it.

What happens when I run out of hearts?

Your heart panel shows the time remaining until a full refill. You can wait for the one-hour recovery or use the test checkout to refill five hearts for 5 simulated GEL. Test balance is not real money.

Will a test payment charge my bank card?

No. LexiGo’s current checkout uses simulated cards and test balance. Do not enter a real card number or security code. You can review plans and test transactions in your account.

Where are my profile and privacy settings?

Your profile includes study preferences and a Privacy section for downloading your data. Account deletion requires your password and, when enabled, a second authentication factor. Staff must resolve unfinished responsibilities first.

YOUR LITTLE WORLD. ONE TAP AWAY.

Keep LexiGo on your Home Screen.

Free to add. The same account, courses and saved progress, in an app-style window.

On iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this website in Safari.
  2. Open Safari’s Share menu, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. If shown, keep Open as Web App enabled. Choose Add.
  4. Open the new LexiGo icon. Sign in with your existing account if asked.

On Android or a computer

Open your browser’s menu and look for “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen”. Availability depends on your browser.

Internet access is required for lessons and account features. This Home Screen version is not an App Store download. There is no separate installation fee; Free and Premium features work as on the website.
